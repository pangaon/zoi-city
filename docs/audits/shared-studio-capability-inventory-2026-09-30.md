## Smallest coherent shared Studio integration

1. Keep current authenticated workspace selection and authoritative owner listing context. Obtain explicit brand/profile facts; never infer a business identity from an uploaded file.
2. Establish one bounded workspace asset lifecycle with actual upload validation, source/rights attribution and private preview. Do not immediately expose draft media through a public bucket URL by default.
3. Reuse generation reservation/history and brand profile for editable text suggestions. Treat media transcription/vision and provider-specific video conversion as separate unavailable stages until implemented and funded.
4. Hand approved text/media variants into the existing composer writer and scheduler with explicit destination capability checks. Persist a campaign-to-post mapping and recover uncertain saves before issuing new ones; do not replace legacy persistence casually.
5. Show each destination's actual state: draft, scheduled, delivered with receipt, failed, or unknown. Never convert “sent to API” into a delivery claim or external engagement metrics.

Customer credits, paid entitlements and BYOK require a reviewed billing/key-storage design before they can be sold or advertised. Existing Stripe availability checks in `social-config/index.ts` do not establish an AI subscription or credit ledger. Native reuse is currently a model/contract opportunity; no new native campaign workflow was tested in this audit.
