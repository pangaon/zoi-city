# Specialist assignments

Select the role relevant to the work. These are responsibilities, not extra
concurrent agents or permission to duplicate an existing suite.

## Experience and SaaS organization

Own task hierarchy, typography, spacing, contrast, responsive layouts and complete
interaction states. Build progressive owner setup, contextual help and optional
walkthroughs. Review dialogs and forms in use. Make glass/motion serve readability.
Keep the customer journey and the owner's operational journey connected.

## Restaurant, bakery and hospitality

Check identity, actual imagery, address, timezone, conflicting hours, menus,
price/currency, dietary claims, ordering, reservations, group events and promotions.
Check owner item editing and explicit removal. Hotels and rentals require actual
dates, capacity/inventory, policies, price and confirmation from a connected system;
otherwise provide a real provider handoff or enquiry, with no invented reservation.

## Parish and community organizations

Distinguish same-named parishes by exact ID, city, address and jurisdiction. Check
parish photography, services, timezone, calendar tradition, namedays, family/youth
programmes, ministries, volunteering and monastery affiliations. Reference feasts
are not parish-published service times. Seasonal visuals respect actual dates and
image identity. Youth enrolment and contact data require the existing guardian and
private access controls. Nameday greetings need reviewed recipients and real send
receipts before claiming bulk messaging.

## Artists, creators and professionals

Check the person's identity separately from their publisher, association or channel.
Artist homes need available recordings, gallery, official videos, shows and relevant
booking/contact routes. Creator homes need real channels and collaboration journeys.
Professional homes need actual discipline, credentials, languages, location and
services. Preserve source-specific designs while supporting all members of a family.
Do not mistake external provider playback or a social link for account integration.

## Community, media and maps

Check creation, upload, playback, sharing, reactions, following, notifications and
privacy as complete journeys. Floating media remains associated with the selected
artist across navigation; respects close, resize, focus and mobile safe areas.
Maps need useful hover/focus/touch previews, selected-place actions, sensible zoom
and user-controlled location. Spoken place summaries are not live route guidance.
Hyperlocal editorial editions must preserve geographic scope and sponsorship truth.

## Platform, enrichment and independent acceptance

Check public projections, moderation, duplicates, canonical routes, source identity,
SEO/schema, provider configuration, performance and failure recovery. Preserve
owner data and shared pipeline invariants. Review the actual deployed artifact and
individual checklist. A source fetch is not a finished home, a screenshot is not a
transaction test, and a successful example is not site-wide acceptance.
