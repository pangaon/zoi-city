# Founder visual direction — October 16, 2026

The founder supplied concept references for the welcome, member home, restaurant
owner overview, Studio, publishing, website tools, analytics, barber, events,
BuyGreek, creator portfolio, Explore map, sector homes and Community/reels/connections. These steer the entire existing delivery scope.
A welcome image alone does not satisfy the requirement.

## Shared visual language

- Use the exact approved blue Zoi emblem. Keep the installed app named **Zoi**.
- Pair deliberate editorial serif display type with readable system body and
  control type. The references favour deep navy ink, bright cobalt actions,
  luminous ivory/white surfaces and atmospheric dark client/creative rooms.
- Let strong photographs and individual identity establish the scene. Preserve
  correct image proportions, a useful crop and an unobstructed subject.
- Use restrained glass, fine borders, soft depth, rounded geometry and a clear
  selected state. Glass must have an opaque accessible fallback, especially for
  reduced transparency and low power. Maintain contrast over every photograph.
- Keep hierarchy compact and intentional. Show the useful next action; avoid
  repeated logos, generic pastel icon grids, oversized empty blocks and a large
  toolbar consuming the phone viewport.
- Camera movement, reveals, selection and transitions should feel coherent.
  Opening the app must not require watching a timed introduction. Respect live
  reduced-motion changes, backgrounding and low power; keep text steady.
- Controls remain native, sharp and functional. Preserve safe areas, large text,
  keyboard focus, readable labels, scrolling and targets of at least 44 points.

## Reference-specific outcomes

**Welcome:** bright Cycladic architecture, sea and diaspora atmosphere; centered
brand, “Life, together.” and “Your people. Your possibilities.”; an accessible
glass action panel. Explore opens the member home without sign-in. Sign in opens
the existing account journey. Community, Discover and Create choose their actual
destinations. Privacy opens the public policy with recovery on failure. A
language selector requires actual translated behaviour before it is advertised.

**Member home:** image-led editorial discovery, an economical context bar,
readable segmented choices where their data and behaviour exist, purposeful
everyday entry points, actual community content and polished persistent navigation.
Never fabricate the member's city, notification count, social activity or feed.

**Client homes:** the barber's photographic treatment, services, prices, staff
and time selection differ from a creator's portrait, work, journal, collaborations
and media kit. Apply the same care across populated and sparse records in every
family. Keep the client’s visually verified branding, source content and useful
operating journey; the reference client names are illustrative.

**Owner experience:** real client context, a useful attention queue, correct
period/source-labelled metrics and relevant next actions. Studio has a composed
working canvas, real media/brand controls, saved draft/recovery, review and
distribution. Publishing shows actual connection/scheduling states. Website
tools show evidenced findings, previews and applied corrections. Analytics shows
real measured activity and explicit unavailable data.

**Events:** immersive organizer/artist imagery, accurate venue/timezone/date,
clear overview/seat/detail hierarchy, readable seating states and a persistent
ticket review action. Use actual venue plans and inventory/holds. The sample
Harbour Room, six tables and $85 price do not establish a real event or stock.
Preserve all existing Signature venue-specific requirements.

**BuyGreek:** an editorial native catalogue with actual product photography,
working search/collections/options, readable prices with currency, a clear bag
and thoughtful product/maker presentation. Preserve the canonical Shopify cart
and checkout session. Concept ceramics, brands, prices and bag counts must never
be published as the store's inventory. A product handoff is not an accepted cart
write, payment, order or fulfillment result.

**Explore/map:** a legible full map with location/search and meaningful category
filters, selected-place card, Map/List selection, profile and directions actions.
Maintain exact real record identity between marker, card, profile and navigation.
A decorative city map or an invented precise pin does not satisfy the reference.

**Sector editions:** barber service/staff/time selection, accommodation dates/guest
count/room inventory and restaurant menu/reservation journeys require different
composition, typography, colour accents and actual operations. Keep Zoi navigation
coherent while giving each client a distinct home. Reference NIKOS, THALASSA HOUSE
and ELIA identities, staff, facilities and prices are fictional examples.

**Community and connections:** compact author/context header, readable photo/video
posts, relevant real business/event links, creation and relationship actions;
immersive reels with actual playback, pause/progress/captions where available;
people, opportunities and articles with useful search and real contextual actions.
Following, Nearby and For you must correspond to actual audience/location/ranking
logic and privacy settings. Do not populate illustrative people, posts, likes,
collaborations or messages as real community activity.

## Acceptance

Use actual phone/tablet screens and exercise each control. Check light/dark
presentation, large text, Greek/long names, absent imagery, loading, error/retry,
account switches and interrupted outcomes. Source, controlled layout, actual
rendered app, installed device and provider results remain distinct evidence.
Generated atmospheric brand artwork must have provenance and must not be
represented as factual client photography. Full client and operational coverage
remains in the existing authoritative execution queue.
