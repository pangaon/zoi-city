# Adult football operations — candidate

No existing club is labelled a league automatically. Olympic Flame is a club; KOPA is a league-level listing requiring its owner's configuration. No active seasons, teams, fixtures or results are seeded. Adult teams only; no players, minors, birth dates or federation affiliation collected/claimed. This is a competition workflow, not a verified governing body or prediction service.

Owner/admin only for each workspace. All writes optimistic-versioned or new stable UUID exact-payload idempotent; use fresh read after uncertain versioned mutation. Security-definer RPCs enforce ownership/current public visibility; direct tables private RLS/no client grants. Contact_notes only organizer and that club; other teams cannot see pending applicants or contact details. Audit organizer-private.

- `football_clubs(p_query text='')` public → `{ok,clubs:[{id,name,slug,city,country,entity_type}]}` actual owned public sports/organization/business listings, max30 name matches. This does not assert a business is a licensed club; organizer decides suitability.
- `football_public(p_host uuid|null=null,p_season uuid|null=null)` public → `{ok,seasons,season,teams,fixtures,rules}`. Published/current host only; season list max30; a selected season's accepted/current-public teams and published fixtures only. Public team `{id,club_id,name,club_name,club_slug}`. Fixture `{id,home_id,away_id,starts_at,ends_at,timezone,venue,status,home_score,away_score}`; scores null unless confirmed. Fixed rules win3/draw1/loss0, tie break points/GD/GF; shared sporting rank on complete tie, deterministic UUID order for display. Public route `/leagues/?season=UUID&host=UUID`.
- `football_operator(p_workspace uuid,p_season uuid|null=null)` → `{ok,seasons,owned,season,teams,fixtures,audit,can_organize}`. Relevant latest100 seasons; owned public eligible host/club listings `{id,name,entity_type}`. Season full row below. Teams full rows for organizer or own club; other accepted participants receive only id/season_id/club_id/name/status/club_workspace, never contact/adult/private request data. Fixtures full only organizer or participating team's club. Latest100 audit organizer-only.
- `football_season_save(p_workspace,p_id,p_expected_version,p_data)` → `{ok,season}`. `{host_id,host_kind:'club'|'league',name,timezone,starts_on,ends_on,published}`. Name1..160, timezoneIANA, dateISO range<=730days. NewUUID expected0, exact initial retry. Draft by default in UI. Max30 seasons/workspace. Host/kind/timezone locked after teams exist; dates cannot exclude existing noncancelled fixtures. Season `{id,workspace_id,host_id,host_kind,name,timezone,starts_on,ends_on,published,version,initial_data,created_by,created_at,updated_at}`.
- `football_team_submit(p_workspace,p_season,p_id,p_data,p_invite=false)` → `{ok,team}`. Data `{club_id,name,contact_notes,adult_confirmed}`. Name1..120, notes<=2500. Self-application requires current owner/admin of club, published season and explicit adult_confirmed true. Organizer invitation may target existing owned public club but remains invited until that club owner/admin explicitly accepts adult invitation; organizer cannot fabricate club acceptance. NewUUID exactdata retry, max64 rows/season. Team `{id,season_id,club_id,club_workspace,name,contact_notes,adult_confirmed,invited,status,version,initial_data,created_by,created_at,updated_at}`.
- `football_team_decide(p_workspace,p_team,p_expected_version,p_action)` → `{ok,team}`. Club accept_adult_invitation invited→applied; organizer approve applied→accepted; organizer decline invited/applied→declined; club withdraw invited/applied/accepted→withdrawn only if no noncancelled fixtures. Existing played season history must be resolved by organizer, not silently deleted by club withdrawal.
- `football_fixture_save(p_workspace,p_season,p_id,p_expected_version,p_data)` → `{ok,fixture}`. Organizer only. `{home_id,away_id,starts_at,ends_at,timezone,venue,published,cancelled?:boolean,reason?:string}`. Distinct accepted adult teams; explicit finite ISO timestamps in season source timezone; duration>0<=8hours and within season dates; manually entered venue1..500. Season row serializes all fixture creates/edits, preventing simultaneous overlap involving same team. New expected0+exactretry, max1000 fixtures. Edit scheduled only. Existing fixture can be cancelled with reason1..1000; confirmed cancelled result drops from standings but audit retained. No automatic calendar event or venue reservation.
- `football_score_submit(p_workspace,p_fixture,p_expected_version,p_home,p_away)` → `{ok,fixture}`. Organizer or participating club, only after scheduled end, scores integer0..99; scheduled/disputed→proposed. Does not update standings.
- `football_score_decide(p_workspace,p_fixture,p_expected_version,p_action:'confirm'|'dispute',p_reason='')` → `{ok,fixture}`. Participating opposite club or organizer, and both different actor/profile and different workspace from proposal. A submitted organizer score needs a participating club; a team proposal needs opponent or independent organizer. Proposed→confirmed/disputed; dispute reason1..1000. Disputed scores excluded until replacement proposal independently confirmed. Same workspace owning all parties cannot self-confirm results.

Fixture full row `{id,season_id,home_id,away_id,starts_at,ends_at,timezone,venue,published,status,home_score,away_score,proposed_by,proposed_workspace,confirmed_by,decision_reason,version,initial_data,created_at,updated_at}`. Identity uses zoi_me.profile.id, never assumes auth UID equality. Public season basic fields id/host_id/host_kind/name/timezone/starts_on/ends_on (+host_name/host_slug in list). Football host/club ownership changes invalidate public views/current writes; old private history remains original workspace. Overlap checks scope the actual registered season team ID; cross-season rosters and athlete clashes are unknown. Existing adult group membership may be used independently; not linked or counted as a team roster here.

Stable operator deep link `/social/leagues/?season=UUID` opens a public competition application even outside the latest season list. Public fixture rendering pages50, operator pages25 while all bounded fixture data are available to calculate standings.
