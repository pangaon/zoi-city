# Entertainment and venue homes

`renderEventHome({entity,template='concierge',design={},reviewMode=false})` is a pure renderer. Set `body.dataset.template`, load `style.css`, then call `mountEventActions(entity)` once; returned cleanup aborts listeners. Four stable templates: atelier, concierge, table, parea. Review routes include comparison navigation; canonical render omits it. Copy is escaped. Known section IDs are intro, offerings, media, gallery, contact, socials; section order is deduplicated and intro cannot be hidden. Hidden sections remove their primary/navigation anchors.

Source entity fixtures are `showcase/entertainment/signatureproductions-6aa61d/source.json` and `showcase/venue/parkview-manor-banquet-hall-north-york/source.json`. These preserve separate identities and source provenance; no workspace ownership or provider activation is inferred. Required common keys: id, slug, family, name, eyebrow, headline, intro, website, contact_url, hero, source_checked_at, socials[{label,url}]. Entertainment requires event_url, poster, floorplan. Venue requires menu_url, tour_url, gallery, address, phone, phone_label. All image URLs are verified official original assets. Remote links are HTTPS. The current entertainment content and table coordinate dataset are specifically the verified Signature 20 March2027 concert; do not reuse its event/plan for unrelated businesses. The venue services currently correspond to Parkview's verified services. Use further verified datasets before broader application.
