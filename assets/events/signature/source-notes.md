prefill or submission. No inventory, holds, bottlecatalogue, payment or cash
confirmation is simulated. No localStorage of guest names. No public seed event.

backend cannot be assumed. An optional save callback binds the existing private event-plan adapter. This creates
a private planning record only, never a reservation or paid ticket.

Sponsor-booth labels are the organizer's floorplan category, not sold sponsor ads.
No client logos inserted. Future owner placement flow must bind event/table IDs,
approved artwork/rights, disclosed sponsor identity, safe destination, start/end dates,
capacity/placement rules and approval. Preview may use owner's draft artwork visibly
marked private; public sponsor content appears only from active approved records.
