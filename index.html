What is Zoi?

One platform for the Greek world: free software for Greek businesses, a worldwide marketplace through BuyGreek.Shop, and the definitive map of Greek life across the diaspora.

Yes — signing up, claiming your page, and the core tools are free to start. Paid features like ticket sales use standard payment processing.

How do I claim my business?

Find it in the directory, tap claim, verify ownership. Once approved, the page is yours — description, hours, links, photos.

The Greek marketplace — multi-vendor commerce where Greek businesses sell products, tickets and services to the diaspora worldwide.

Who is it for?

Greek businesses, professionals, creators, organizers and churches — and every member of the diaspora looking for their people and places.
