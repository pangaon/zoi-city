Your information, explained

Privacy at Zoi.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Zoi’s website and mobile apps, including connected features such as maps and the BuyGreek store.

Updated 16 October 2026 · Contact: pangaon@gmail.com

InformationHow it is usedConnected servicesYour choicesRetention

Information you provide

When you create or use an account, Zoi processes your email address, account identifier and the profile details you choose to provide, such as your name, photo, biography, links or locality. Authentication services process the credentials needed for sign-in.

Information you submit through a feature can include posts, replies, messages, photos, videos, enquiries, event participation and business workspace records. The information involved depends on the features you use and your workspace permissions. Public posts and profile fields are visible to other people; private workspace information is subject to the workspace’s access controls.

Business and organization listings can also contain information supplied by their owners or obtained from public sources. Contact us if information about you is incorrect.

How information is used

Information is processed to provide your account and selected features, display published content, manage workspace access, respond to enquiries, operate the service and investigate abuse or technical problems.

Like other online services, our hosting and service providers process network requests, which can include an IP address, device or browser information, request details and service logs. These support delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Device permissions and local storage

Map location access is requested when you choose to use your device position. You can browse places without granting location permission. Map requests can reveal the area being displayed to the services that load the map and nearby results.

Media features may request access to a photo, video, camera or microphone when you choose a feature that needs it. Manage these permissions in your device settings.

Zoi stores sign-in credentials, preferences and some unfinished drafts on your device so your session and work can continue. The embedded store also maintains its own browsing session and cookies. Signing out, clearing browser data or removing an app can affect local information.

Services that help Zoi work

Zoi uses service providers for hosting, authentication, database and file storage, maps and other selected functionality. Current core infrastructure includes Vercel and Supabase. A provider receives information needed to handle the requests made to its service. Information may be processed in countries other than your own.

When you open BuyGreek inside the app, its Shopify storefront processes store browsing and shopping information. If you place an order, the store, seller and payment or delivery providers process the contact, shipping, order and payment information needed for that transaction. Zoi’s native app does not ask you to email payment details. Store accounts and orders are separate from your Zoi account.

Opening a linked website, music or video provider, directions service or checkout sends a request to that provider. Their privacy terms also apply. Information you deliberately share with a business, organizer or other member is received by that recipient.

Authorized workspace members may access information associated with their roles. Information may also need to be disclosed to meet a legal obligation, protect people or investigate misuse of the service.

Your choices and requests

You can browse public parts of Zoi without signing in. Choose which optional profile details and content you publish, and use available edit, removal, report and blocking controls. Your device settings let you change permissions.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact pangaon@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. Explain what you would like to access, correct or delete. We may verify your identity before acting. Rights and any lawful exceptions depend on your location and the information concerned.

Deleting a Zoi account does not automatically cancel an order with a seller or delete an account held with a separate provider. Please contact that provider about its records.

Retention and security

Account and submitted information is retained to operate the features you use and manage the account. Some transaction, business, security, dispute or legal records may need to be kept after an account is closed. The applicable period depends on the type of record and the reason it is needed; contact us about a specific record or deletion request.

Access controls and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep sign-in codes and passwords private, and report suspected unauthorized access to support.

Family features and policy updates

Family and youth administration tools are intended for authorized adults managing those activities. Avoid publishing children’s personal information. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate authority.

We may update this policy as features or practices change. The date above identifies the current version. For questions about this policy or Zoi’s handling of information, contact pangaon@gmail.com.
