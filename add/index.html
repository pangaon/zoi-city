Reading your site…

01

You paste the address

Only a real website. We will not accept an internal or private address, and we check again the moment before we connect.

02

We read the page

Structured data and the tags your site already publishes. One request, eight second limit, your robots.txt respected.

03

You confirm

Everything we found is shown with where it came from. Nothing publishes until you say so.

04

You prove it is yours

A separate step, because a page loading does not prove you own the business. That is what makes a verified mark worth anything.

What we will not do

We will not invent a field. If your site does not publish a phone number, the field stays empty rather than being filled with a guess.

We will not mark you verified because your site loaded. A successful fetch proves the site exists — not that it is yours.

We will not overwrite anything you type. Machine-read values live in their own namespace and yours always win.
