Zoi support

A little help, right when you need it.

Questions about your account, a place you found or something that isn’t working? Send us the details so we can help.

Email Zoi support ↗pangaon@gmail.com

Include the page or profile link and what happened. For app issues, include the version shown in TestFlight or your app store. Keep passwords, sign-in codes and payment details out of your message.

Signing in

Use the email address for your Zoi account. You can sign in with your password or request an email code. Check your spam folder if the code doesn’t arrive, and use the newest code.

If you’re still stuck, email us from that address. We’ll never ask you to send your password or sign-in code.

A place or map needs a correction

Send the Zoi profile link, the correct street address and an official website or other source we can check. Tell us if the business has moved or has several branches.

You can explore the map without granting device location. Turn location access on only when you want to use your position.

BuyGreek orders

BuyGreek shopping opens the official store. For an existing order, start with the contact details in your order confirmation so the store or seller can identify it.

Include your order number when contacting the seller. Don’t email card numbers or bank details. Zoi support can help with opening the store in the app.

Community and safety

Use the report control on a post or member when available, and block or mute a member to manage what you see. You can also email us with a link to the content and a short explanation.

Avoid including someone else’s private information in a public post or report attachment.

Your business workspace

Sign in with the account that has access to the business. Select the correct workspace before opening its tools. Your role determines which settings you can change.

If access looks wrong, send the business link and the email you use for Zoi. Don’t share another team member’s credentials.

Privacy and account requests

To ask about your information, correct it or request account deletion, email us from the address linked to your account. We may need to verify that the account is yours.

Tell us which account or information the request concerns. Business records and orders may have separate retention requirements.
